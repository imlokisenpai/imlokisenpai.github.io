They all say I have changed. They do not know I have always been this way.

Rage, buried alive in a coffin, shall rise when judgment comes. Tears, hidden for far too long, shall turn to blood in furious veins, behind eyes blurred by the weight of it all.

Sadness, imprisoned within the heart, slowly turns it into cold stone.

I have not changed. I was never better then, either. Back then, I was merely a vessel for rage. Now, I am the rage itself.

I carved a lake from my tears, only to watch it dry. Now, nothing remains but the bitter salt of everything I wept.

Even sadness has abandoned me. For how can a heart of stone carry the weight of sorrow?

I am who I have always been. Back then, it was a reflection of me. A shadow cast by something I refused to see.

But now, there is no reflection left.

There is no shadow. No mask. No distance between what I am and what the world sees.

I have not changed.

I have merely become myself.
