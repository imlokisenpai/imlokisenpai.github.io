Oh mother, please forgive me,
I will not suffer anymore…

Shadows call my name softly,
Like they’ve known me from before.

I walk where the light is fading,
Through a door I can’t ignore…

Every breath feels like a memory,
Falling broken to the floor.

If sorrow had a heartbeat,
It would echo in my core…

Oh mother, please forgive me,
I’m not the child you prayed for.

The night wraps its arms around me,
Saying I don’t need to fight no more…

Let the world forget my footsteps,
Let the wind erase my lore.

And if tomorrow never finds me,
Know I loved you evermore…
