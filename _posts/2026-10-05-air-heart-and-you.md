note: |-
  I have never understood the need to justify love.
  Some feelings arrive before reason does, and asking why they exist feels almost like asking the heart why it beats.
  This is not a poem about perfect love.
  It is about the kind of love that becomes indistinguishable from being alive.
  Perhaps that is its beauty.
  Perhaps that is its danger.
tags: ["love","heart","air"]
poem: true

I need no reason to love you.
Loving you is the air my lungs remember
take it away, and breath becomes a lie.

Love with conditions? What a joke.
The heart doesn’t ask permission to beat,
and I don’t ask permission to love you.

Madly in love
I’ve not been sane since our eyes collided.

For me, loving you is being alive.
If my love ends, so do I.
I may keep breathing,
exist by name alone,
or live as a soul in fragments
it makes no difference.
