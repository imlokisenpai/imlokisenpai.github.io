Never had words to name my feelings;
thus, words became meaningless.
Everything I felt, I felt too deeply.
Thoughts so beautiful that putting them into words
would strip them of their charm.
And so, my love remained silent.

Like a tree loving the children who play in its shade,
the shade is there,
and so is the love:
quiet,
caring,
constant.

A love so deep and vast
that no words can define its sincerity.

And like a flower that knows its end,
yet blooms at the first touch of sunlight,
even knowing the last ray may be its last.

So was I,
unable to speak.
Because love in its purest form
was never meant to be said.
It is felt.
