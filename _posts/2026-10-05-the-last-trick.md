Even the god of tricks fell in love,
And all his tricks came to an end.

Punished for the debts of fate,
Bound beneath a serpent's gaze,
I waited for the venom to fall.

Yet you were there.

You cupped the poison in your hands,
As though my suffering belonged to you,
As though every drop that sought my heart
Had first to pass through your devotion.

And suddenly,

Even chains felt gentle,
Even the serpent seemed distant,
Even damnation resembled heaven
When you stood before me.

The earthquakes of my nature,
The tremors of my anger,
The ruin left in the wake of my flaws,
Never shook your resolve.

You remained.

Patient as the stars,
Steadfast as the roots of the world tree,
Loving me through every storm I became.

Thus the trickster met the only force
He could neither deceive nor escape.

And so Loki fell,

Not to gods,
Not to fate,
Not to punishment,

But to love.

His final trick
Was believing he could outrun it.
