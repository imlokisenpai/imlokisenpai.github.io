  This was written for my mother.

  She was blamed for misfortunes she never caused, judged for the kindness that once defined her, and left to carry wounds she did not deserve.

  In the end, she chose silence.

  I do not know what waits beyond it.
  I only know I wanted her to rest.

  Perhaps that is why I rejected the gods.

I wanted her to rest for eternity,
so I rejected the gods.
She was beautiful and kind,
yet she suffered
for caring too much,
betrayed for doing good,
for loving those she loved.
Still, she was blamed
blamed for misfortune,
the same misfortune
they stitched with their own hands.
At last, she found comfort,
not in heaven,
but in fire
a final silence,
a rest they never gave her in life.
Now they want her back,
to wash their sins
in the name of the gods.
So I rejected the gods.
