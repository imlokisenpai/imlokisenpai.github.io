Have you ever heard of the great loves?

A hero who, after his wife's death,
could never find another woman beautiful.

A man who lost his love to riches,
and could never look at another woman the same way.

A child who grew old,
yet could never forget his childhood playmate,
his first love, his soulmate.

People who cannot love another
the way they once loved one person,
giving every ounce of themselves
to a single soul.

I think about that sometimes.

But I am no hero.

I am not the kind of man
who will crave one person
until the end of the earth.

I find beauty in other women too.
I notice their statuesque curves,
the shape of their bodies,
the things that make a man look twice.

Yet I cannot find the solace
I once found in your arms.

I cannot see a blue ocean in their eyes.
Their eyes look still to me,
not a ripple of that spark
I once saw in yours.

I am someone in between.

I find them attractive,
yet I do not want to touch them.

My heart does not race
the way it did with you.

And sometimes I ask myself,

what kind of love is this?

I cannot be one of those
great lovers of the past.

And I cannot lie to myself either.

Solace is found in the arms of the one we love.
The spark of beauty may fade with time,
but the ache of wanting you
has not learned how to fade.

Perhaps that is what remains of us.

I can admire beauty without loving it.
I can desire without reaching for it.

Because my heart has learned something
my eyes cannot seem to forget:

Beauty is everywhere.

But I cannot find beauty
that feels like you.

And perhaps that is my kind of love.

-----
This is not about finding no one beautiful after you. It is about realizing that attraction and love are not the same. I can see beauty in others, but I cannot find the same solace, spark, or connection I found in you.
