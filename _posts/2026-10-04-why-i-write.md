Sometimes I ask myself,
why do I even write?

What does writing a letter mean
when I can simply call you?

I think I remained an old soul,
stuck in this future.

And maybe my words are not loyal to me,
but my writing is.

When I write,
I cannot stop describing your beauty.

And when I speak,
my words cannot keep up with my feelings.

When I cry,
the tears don't come out.

But when I write,
my heart bleeds.

When I think,
I lie to myself.

But when I write,
my words cut deep into my soul.

I guess writing makes me know myself.

Even if these letters
are someday going to burn
or fall into the abyss,

I will burn with them
and become as empty as the abyss.

Because I am true in my writing,

and perhaps only an image of myself
in reality.
