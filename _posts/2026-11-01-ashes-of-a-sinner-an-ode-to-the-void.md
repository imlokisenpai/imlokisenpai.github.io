I'm a sinner.

There will come a day when I turn to ash.

But will my sins turn to ash with me,
Or will they outlive the flesh that carried them?

My kindness died long before I did.
No one remembered it,
Not until all that remained of me
Was merely bones and flesh.

Then why must a man be punished?
When the world itself is a hell,
With not a glimpse of peace in sight.

What is peace, if I am destined to become ash?
What is hell,
When the world has already mastered cruelty?

I've wanted it all to end
Since the day I became conscious.
I seek no peace,
Nor do I care for the sins I've committed.

After all, I'll turn to ash sooner or later.

From ash I rose,
And to ash I shall return.

There are no sins in ash.
No kindness, no cruelty.
No heaven, no hell.
No peace to seek,
Nor punishment to fear.

Only stillness.

That is all I ever wanted.

For my consciousness to dissolve into the void,
For everything I was to cease to be.

My sins and my kindness,
My virtues and my vices,
All reduced to the same nothingness.

For in ash, nothing is guilty.
