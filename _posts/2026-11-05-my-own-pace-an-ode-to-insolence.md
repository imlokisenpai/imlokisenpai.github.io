I was an unruly child,
The notion of being out of line
Was always in my blood.

I never cared for recognition,
Nor did I care much for what I was doing.

I did what I believed in,
And lived the life I chose.

People come and go,
Each carrying their own gospel,
Wishing to carve their beliefs into your mind.

To them, their way is the right way,
And somehow, it ought to be everyone's.

A bunch of selective sinners,
That's what I call them.

They abstain from meat on Tuesdays,
As if their God turns a blind eye
To the blood spilled on every other day.

I care for no one's approval,
Save that of one man.

The man who raised me.

And if he says I'm right,
Who the fuck cares what your opinions are?
I never really asked for them.

Cry, cry, and fill the river,
I've grown accustomed to its salty waters.

Let your blood boil at my insolence,
I'll drink it with some bread.

My father told me,
"Walk ten steps back,
But always walk at your own pace.
Aim less, and live more."

And that's the only rule
I've ever cared to follow.
