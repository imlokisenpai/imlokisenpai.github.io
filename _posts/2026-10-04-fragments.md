For my whole life, I've been dreaming.

The same dream I first dreamed
in that dark corner of my room.

Dreams of being.
Being me again.
Being the whole person
I was meant to be.

Tracing every piece that fell apart,
collecting them one by one,
my broken hands drawing blood
with every shard I picked up.

And when I finally became
the whole picture,

it was tasteless.

There was nothing left to imagine,
no fractures left to mend,
no room for becoming.

And it disgusted me.

I disgusted myself

for never becoming
the person I had dreamed of

in that dark corner
of my room.
