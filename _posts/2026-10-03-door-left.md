What arrow is it
if the strings are not held by the hands of your loved one?

And what scar is it
if it was not received as a pardoning gift
from the one who was loyal?

Pain, suffering, and heartbreak
are meaningless
unless they are inflicted by the hand
you once gave permission
to caress your hair.

A door you never opened
will never hurt you.
And a door you have closed
will never understand.

It is the fate of the brave,
those who dared to give someone
a place within their beating chest.

The siege of pain
always comes for the brave,
the ones who kept their doors open
to shelter the ones they loved.

And perhaps that is life:

to keep people close,
even when they hold
the strings of your fate.

For a door that is never opened
may remain untouched,
but what life is there
behind a door that refuses to open?
