I cannot get rid of the chair.

This is not sentiment. I want to be accurate about this, because everyone has
a story about why people keep things and mine is not that one.

The chair is not *theirs*. It is a chair. It was in the flat before, it was in
the flat after, and when I finally moved I carried it eleven blocks in the rain
like a person carrying a grudge, and it is not special.

But the room is wrong without it.

That is the whole of it. The room has a space in it now — an absence with a
shape — and every other absence in the room has been absorbed into that one,
the way a small stone gathers the sand around it. If I take the chair out, the
space will still be there. It will simply be a room where someone used to sit,
which is somehow a much colder sentence.

Everyone says: *you'll know when you're ready.*

I have been ready for a year. I think what I am actually waiting for is to be
over it, and those are not the same, and no one tells you that because it would
sound like an excuse.

So the chair stays. Not as a memorial. As furniture.
