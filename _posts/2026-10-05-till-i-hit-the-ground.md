If I were to be a poet
You would be the silence after the words
And if I were to be a fish
You would be the flow
A poet without admirers may survive,
But without silence he won’t know himself,
And a fish without the flow
Is certain of death.
I have been the most carefree person,
A leaf falling from the tree,
Floating in the wind,
Cut from life yet beautiful.
I was like that once too
Broken, filled with sadness or perhaps something else,
I was never good at figuring it out,
But like the wind, you made me beautiful.
Even if I can’t be like the seed,
Grow into something new,
Your wind made me feel enough.
I hope you will be with me like the wind,
At least till I hit the ground.
