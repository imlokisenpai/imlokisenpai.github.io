Her hair, like ocean waves,
Curling into themselves,
Each little coil resembling a wolf's ear.

Those soft, puffy cheeks,
As delicate as cotton to the touch;
Her big, round eyes,
And those little hands,
With so little grasp,
Yet somehow capable of holding the world.

Every little detail,
Engraved in my mind and etched into my soul.
Her laughter, the curves of her body,
Every contour mapped into my memory
By nothing more than a single touch.

So gentle, so sweet,
That I could surrender myself
To your tender embrace for eternity.

Lips unlike any I have ever known,
Pink as cherry blossoms in spring.
Only my soul knows how grateful I am
To have made yours my first.

I could spend eternities counting
All the ways you are beautiful,
All the ways you take my breath away,
How love and lust can coexist,
Entwined so deeply
That both threaten to devour my soul.

You, my lady, are something else.
An angel born into this human world,
A petal caught in a waterfall,
So charismatic, so charming,
So beautifully full of meaning.

And here I am,
A nihilist trying to find meaning
In a world that has never promised any.

Yet, when I look at you,
I find myself questioning everything I once believed.

How can something so beautiful exist
Without the intervention of a God?

Perhaps I have spent my life searching
For a reason to believe in something.

And perhaps,
For once,
I have found one.
