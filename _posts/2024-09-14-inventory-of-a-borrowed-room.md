one chair, borrowed
one cup with a chip that catches the lip
a window that does not quite close
the smell of someone else's dinner

a book I never opened
still open, at page ninety,
a hair caught in the spine

six weeks of dust
arranged by someone
into a shape I could not name

I left with the cup
and the window's complaint
and the word *borrowed*,
which I have not returned.
