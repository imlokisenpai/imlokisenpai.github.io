the house was the right shape
but the doors were in the wrong order
and I did not mind

there was water in the kitchen
at the height of the table
and it had the stillness of a decision

my mother was in the next room
being careful about something
I could hear the care

I opened a cupboard and inside
was every drawer of every desk
I have ever owned, all at once,
labelled, and I understood
that the labels were for me

outside, the street
was dry, which was wrong,
and I thought: good, I was right,
and then I woke up
and the relief went first,
before the dream had finished leaving.
