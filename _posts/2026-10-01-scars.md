Never seen a good man without scars,
Be they scars of war
Or scars of life.

Scars where shields couldn't reach,
And scars that love couldn't heal.

Once, I looked into my father's eyes,
Torn and weak from within,
A weakness no glass could hide.

Flesh bitten into pieces,
Be it by hounds
Or by family.

He was taught to protect himself
From the arrows of enemies,
Not from the backstabs of time.

He was strong once,
Held his ground like a warrior.

Then age came,
And his legs began to crumble too.

His back bent forward
From carrying responsibilities alone,
Responsibilities meant for a hundred men.

Still, I see a glimpse of hope
In those old eyes.

A hope for his children,
A hope burning for me...

And I will burn like him,
Like every man does,
Until he can.
