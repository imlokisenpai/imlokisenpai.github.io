COLOPHON

This archive was never meant to become a book.

It is only what remained after the conversations ended,
the letters were never sent,
the anger became quiet,
and the thoughts had nowhere else to go.

Some things here are memories.
Some are inventions.

I no longer remember which is which.

Some pages were written because I had something to say.

Others because I had nowhere to put what I felt.

A few were written in anger.
A few in love.
Some in grief.
Some simply because silence became unbearable.

Some pages are unfinished.

They will remain that way.

There is no particular lesson here.

No reliable narrator.
No carefully constructed meaning.
No promise that any of it is true.

Only the record of having felt something
and having left evidence behind.

If you found this archive by accident,

perhaps it was not meant for you.

But you are here now.

So read carefully.

---

AUTHOR

Loki

---

The archive remains open.

The pages remain restless.

Nothing here has been put to rest.
